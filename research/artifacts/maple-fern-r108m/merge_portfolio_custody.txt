  Every P(>=1 of 2) above assumes two independent draws. Two submissions
  of an UNCHANGED tree are content-deduplicated (freeze protocol 4.2,
  proven from /usr/local/libexec/mlxfast.js): the second call returns
  'Submission already exists', result.job is null, no scoring job is
  created, and the note is discarded. Two draws against one tree is one
  draw. So the honest column, absent a defeat, is P(1 draw).
